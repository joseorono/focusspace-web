Enter FocusSpace: Your digital workspace reset button. We built a completely open-source, privacy-first tool that instantly transforms your chaotic browser into a zen-like environment optimized for focus.

Feature 1: One-Click Session Cleaner
Instantly close distracting tabs using smart keyword filtering. Keep the tabs you need for work, and let FocusSpace automatically clear the noise.

Feature 2: Targeted Browser Cleaner
Remove distraction-related entries from your history (like Social Media, Memes, or NSFW content) without wiping your entire browsing history. Your essential work links stay exactly where they belong.

Feature 3: Built-in Focus Mode
Stay in the zone with an integrated Pomodoro timer, task management, and real-time notifications that gently alert you if you try to open a distracting site during a deep work session.

Add the extension and define your custom rules, keywords, and whitelisted work domains in our intuitive settings panel.

Q: Does this delete all my browsing history?
A: No! FocusSpace only targets specific categories you define, like social media or entertainment, leaving your important work history completely intact.

Q: Is my data sent to any servers?
A: Absolutely not. FocusSpace is privacy-first and 100% open-source. All processing happens locally on your device. Your data never leaves your browser.

Q: Can I customize what gets closed?
A: Yes. You have full control over custom keywords, categories, and whitelisted domains through the smart settings dashboard.

Q: Is it really free?
A: Yes, FocusSpace is completely free and open-source forever. No paywalls, no premium tiers, no tracking.
