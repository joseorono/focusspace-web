## ✅ SOLUTION SECTION

### A Cleaner Browser for a Sharper Mind. 

FocusSpace doesn’t just block sites; it sanitizes your entire browsing environment so you can focus on what actually matters.

#### ⚡ **Session Cleaner**
Instantly close distracting tabs using smart keyword filtering. One click, and the clutter is gone, leaving only your essential work open.

#### 🧹 **Surgical History Scrubbing**
Remove "time-sink" entries from your history (Social Media, Memes, NSFW) without wiping your important research or login sessions. Keep your "Recently Closed" list relevant.

#### 🎯 **Deep Work Focus Mode**
A built-in Pomodoro timer with task management that sends real-time alerts if you wander into distracting territory. It’s like having a digital coach guarding your focus.

#### 🛡️ **Privacy Without Compromise**
No cloud. No tracking. No data collection. All processing happens locally on your device—your data never leaves your browser.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Does it delete my entire browser history?**  
**A:** No. FocusSpace is surgical. You choose which categories (like Social Media or Entertainment) to clean, leaving your useful history and login sessions intact.

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
**A:** 100%. We don't have servers. Everything FocusSpace does happens right in your browser. We couldn't see your data even if we wanted to.

**Q: How much does it cost?**  
**A:** FocusSpace is completely free and open-source. We built it because we needed it, and we want you to have it too.
